## ❓ FAQs

### 🗺️ How big an area can I search?

A city is searched within its own boundary. You can also draw a circle up to a 40 km radius, or a polygon up to 100 km across. Busy areas are split into cells down to 62.5 m, so dense city centres are covered too.

### 🌐 Do I need proxies?

No. Without proxies the app uses your own internet connection for Google Maps, at most 2 requests a second: enough for a town, slow for a big city.

Proxies make it faster. Paste a list in any common format, the app checks each proxy, and every proxy carries 4 requests at a time. Business websites are always read over your own connection.

### 🛑 What if Google blocks my IP address?

The app keeps your own connection to at most 2 requests a second. If Google blocks it anyway, the app pauses it for 30 minutes, then for 60, and goes on more slowly afterwards. After the third block within 24 hours it stops using your connection for the rest of the run.

With proxies, a blocked proxy rests while the others carry on.

### 🔢 What counts as a search? And a lead?

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Search** | One run for one area and one type of business. It counts when it starts. Stopping and resuming it, or restarting the app, counts nothing more. |
| **Lead** | A business the first time it appears in your results on a computer in a calendar month. Found again that month on the same computer, it does not count again. |
| **Review** | Counts against nothing. |

### 🔒 Where is my data?

On your computer, in the app's database: the businesses, contact details and reviews, your proxy list, the areas you draw and the files you export.

Our server receives, for each search, when it started and ended, the app version, how many leads it found and how often Google blocked it. It never receives the area, the type or the businesses. The [FAQ](https://trustscrap.app/en?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme#faq-9) lists everything the app sends.

### 🔄 What if Google changes its pages?

The run stops with "Wait for an update" and a Try again button, rather than carrying on with pages it cannot read. The app shows an Update button when a new version is out, or installs it by itself if you let it.

### 🪟 Windows says "Windows protected your PC". What now?

The Windows installer is not code-signed, so SmartScreen warns before it runs. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### 🍎 Which Macs does it run on?

Every Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple silicon or Intel: the .dmg is universal. The app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

### ⚖️ Who is responsible for the data I collect?

You are. The app collects on your computer, and you decide what it collects and what you do with it, so you answer for it under data-protection law (in the EU, the GDPR).

Use it lawfully, in particular for advertising. In Germany, § 7 UWG requires the recipient's consent for advertising e-mails as a rule, even to businesses. Other countries have their own rules (CAN-SPAM, CASL, PECR).
